Executive Summary

The Templeton family's land centers on the Haden Edwards Survey in Gregg County, with the 173-acre homestead (Property 4) being the big one. Most of the other tracts are carved out of or overlap with that same ground. The mineral interest is still active (barely), with Mary Lou Templeton Sanders getting shut-in royalty checks from Sydri Operating on the Danville Field wells.

Eight properties were identified across four document packages, ranging from a 0.16-acre city lot in Longview to the homestead tract (net ~165 acres after carve-outs). The homestead was lost to a Sheriff's Tax Deed in 1999 (Kilgore ISD v. Luther Templeton), purchased by M&M Investments for $9,272.83. The 1.392-acre partition tract (Property 1) generated the most recorded deed activity and appears to remain in the family chain.

The Danville Field wells (Gregg & Rusk Counties) were shut-in as of March 2023 due to a pipeline force majeure. Current operator is Sydri Operating, LLC (formerly Titan Rock). Shut-in royalty payments of $5 per lease are being issued.
